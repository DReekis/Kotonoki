Kotonoki (言の木)

The quiet public journal and chronological micro-publishing platform.

Kotonoki is built on a simple conviction: the internet does not need another engagement treadmill, algorithmic feed, or creator economy.

It is a space to write something down, place it into a public branch, let others discover it purely chronologically, and leave it behind.

Our Principles

  • Writing comes first: The interface focuses on clear, legible text without distractions.
  • No follower economy: You cannot follow accounts, build audiences, or chase subscriber metrics.
  • No recommendation algorithm: All dispatches appear in pure reverse-chronological order.
  • Self-claimed pen names: No email, phone number, or real-world identity is required.
  • No password recovery: With zero personal data stored, losing your password means that pen name is gone forever.
  • Public shared branches: Categories belong to everyone. Anyone can publish to /cooking, /books, or create a new branch.
  • One image at most: Dispatches are writing documents, not image carousels or galleries.
  • Sticky Desk: Any dispatch can be experienced as a physical memo or printed cleanly as an index card.
Inspired by quiet desktop software and the timeless act of keeping a journal.
